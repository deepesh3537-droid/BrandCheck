# Fictional Demonstration Dataset: Verdant Botanicals

> **Notice:** The brand "Verdant Botanicals", its guidelines, certifications, product descriptions, and employees are entirely fictional. They were authored strictly for demonstration, integration testing, and evaluation of the BrandCheck platform.

## Contents
- `company.json`: Fictional company profile and brand background.
- `sources/`: Approved brand sources entered through structured forms (terms, facts, claims, tone guide).
- `content/with-issues/`: Sample marketing and social drafts containing deliberate brand violations (prohibited terms, fact conflicts, unsupported promotional claims, off-brand tone).
- `content/clean/`: Compliant, authentic content that should pass without false positives.
- `expected-findings.json`: Gold standard expectation dataset for automated testing and evaluation.
