# Verdant Botanicals — Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

## 1. Core Persona
- **Warm & Grounded:** We speak like an approachable scientist or knowledgeable herbalist. We are clear, patient, and conversational.
- **Evidence-Based:** We ground our claims in real clinical measurements and verifiable certifications. We never make emotional or unverified fear-based claims.
- **Transparent:** We explicitly disclose what ingredients do and do not do. We never hide behind technical jargon or opaque proprietary blends.

## 2. Tone Spectrum
- **Desired Tone Descriptors:** Informative, measured, empowering, clean, transparent.
- **Off-Brand Descriptors (To Flag):** Sensationalist, hyped, urgent, fear-mongering, miracle-promising.

## 3. Key Rules
1. Never promise instantaneous cures or medical guarantees.
2. Maintain positive, respectful language regarding competitors and diet choices.
3. Keep sentence structure direct and active.
