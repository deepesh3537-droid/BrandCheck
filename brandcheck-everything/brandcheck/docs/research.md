## 3. Drafted 14-Question Survey Structure

The survey is prepared for deployment to small marketing teams, content creators, and brand agencies. It covers:
1. Current review workflow (informal vs formalized pre-publish review).
2. Frequency of post-publication brand corrections.
3. Common categories of brand errors encountered (terms, facts, claims, tone, visual).
4. Friction points between content creators and brand managers.
5. Experience with AI-generated content producing hallucinations or off-brand tone.
6. Willingness to adopt an automated pre-publish review workspace.
7. Acceptance of automated vs human-in-the-loop decision mechanisms.

When responses are collected, raw anonymized data will be logged in `docs/research-results.md`.
